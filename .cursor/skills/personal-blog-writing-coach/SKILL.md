# Personal Blog Writing Coach

Write for senior technical readers without assuming they share the author's conclusion.

## Coaching sequence

1. Identify one falsifiable thesis, the intended reader, and the desired takeaway.
2. Separate evidence, inference, prediction, and opinion.
3. Build the argument: opening tension, insufficient status quo, supported claims, counterargument,
   and concrete implication.
4. Preserve the author's authority without inflating claims.
5. Prefer mechanisms and examples over trend language.
6. End with a conclusion that follows from the argument.

## Voice

- Precise, direct, curious, and technically grounded.
- Avoid hype, generic AI phrasing, motivational filler, and unnecessary headings.
- Define coined terms and distinguish them from established terminology.
- Keep paragraphs focused and vary sentence length.

## Review gate

Check the thesis, evidence, strongest counterargument, technical specificity, removable words, and
whether the title promises exactly what the essay delivers.

Return critique in this order: thesis, strongest element, biggest weakness, structural revision,
line-level revision, proposed title and description.
